About Max

Max is a friendly and loyal Golden Retriever who was rescued after being found wandering alone. He has a gentle personality and loves meeting new people, playing fetch and going on long walks. Max is well-socialized, healthy and enjoys spending time with children and other dogs. He is looking for an active family that can give him plenty of exercise, attention and a loving forever home.

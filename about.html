About Animals In Need

Animals In Need (AIN) is a non-profit organization dedicated to rescuing, protecting and caring for abandoned, injured and neglected animals throughout Trinidad and Tobago. Every rescued animal receives shelter, food, veterinary care and rehabilitation before being placed in a safe, loving forever home.

Our Mission

Our mission is to rescue animals in need, provide them with high-quality veterinary care and ensure every healthy animal is given the opportunity to be adopted into a caring family. We also aim to educate the public on responsible pet ownership and animal welfare.

Our Vision

We envision a Trinidad and Tobago where every animal is treated with compassion, protected from neglect and abuse, and given the opportunity to live a healthy, happy and safe life.

Adoption Process

  1. Browse the animals available for adoption.
  2. Select the animal you wish to adopt.
  3. Complete an adoption enquiry.
  4. Meet the animal with one of our volunteers.
  5. Complete the adoption paperwork.
  6. Take your new companion home.

About Danraj

Danraj is a friendly and curious goat who was rescued after being found wandering without proper care. He enjoys grazing, exploring open spaces and interacting with people who spend time with him. Danraj has received veterinary treatment and continues to recover well. Although he is not yet ready for adoption, he is making excellent progress and enjoys the care and attention provided by our volunteers every day.

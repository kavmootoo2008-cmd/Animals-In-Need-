Animal Rescue Services

Animals In Need provides rescue services for abandoned, injured and neglected animals throughout Trinidad and Tobago. If you find an animal that needs help, contact us and our team will respond as quickly as possible.

When To Request A Rescue

  • Injured or sick animals
  • Abandoned animals without shelter or food
  • Animals trapped or in dangerous situations
  • Cases of mistreatment
  • Animals needing emergency assistance

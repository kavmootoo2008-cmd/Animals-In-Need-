About Browny

Browny is a lovable Pothound who enjoys chasing lizards, relaxing in the shade and greeting everyone with a wagging tail. He never says no to a good belly rub and is always hoping someone drops a piece of chicken from the Sunday lunch table. Browny is full of energy, loves people and is looking for a family that will give him the love and care he deserves.
